About me

I did my bachelor’s degree in Biology at the University of Central Florida. During my time there I became an author in a research article studying myrmecophiles in the nests of the Florida carpenter ant, Pogonomyrmex badius. Now, I am a graduate student at the University of Puerto Rico Recinto Mayaguez with a focus on entomology and aquatic ecology.
